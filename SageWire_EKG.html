KILLER RHYTHMS
V-Fib DEADLY
Looks like: Chaotic squiggles. No pulse.
Action: DEFIBRILLATE (Shock) immediately + CPR.
V-Tach URGENT
Looks like: Sawtooth / Tombstones.
Action: Check Pulse. If NO pulse -> Shock. If pulse -> Meds (Amiodarone).
Asystole DEADLY
Looks like: Flat line.
Action: CPR + Epinephrine. DO NOT SHOCK (No electricity to reorganize).
A-Fib COMMON
Looks like: Irregularly irregular. Quivering between beats.
Action: Control rate (Diltiazem/Digoxin). Watch for clots.
