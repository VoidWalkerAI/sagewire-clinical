The SageWire Manifesto

This software was forged with a specific philosophy: Sovereignty.

In an era of bloated, fragile, subscription-based technology, SageWire stands apart.

01. ZERO DEPENDENCIES
We do not rely on external servers, cloud libraries, or frameworks that can be deprecated. This code runs on the raw engine of the web. If the internet breaks, this tool still works.

02. ZERO TRACKING
We do not want your data. There are no cookies, no analytics, and no "telemetry." Your patient data never leaves your device.

03. ARCHITECTURE OVER ART
We do not code for aesthetics; we code for utility. Every line of code has a purpose: to save a life or solve a problem.
